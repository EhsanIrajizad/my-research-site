Biostatistics · MD Anderson Cancer Center

Finding cancer earlier, with better statistics.

We build statistical and machine-learning methods that turn blood-based molecular measurements — proteins, metabolites and lipids — into tests that detect cancer sooner, stratify risk more precisely, and anticipate how patients will respond to therapy.
