Lab lead

Ehsan Irajizad

Department of Biostatistics, The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center

Ehsan Irajizad is a biostatistician whose research focuses on the early detection of cancer using blood-based biomarkers. The group develops statistical and machine-learning methods — including approaches that maximise sensitivity at clinically required specificity — and applies them to protein, metabolite and lipid panels for lung, pancreatic, ovarian, breast and other cancers.

Ehsan came to cancer research from biophysics, with early first-author work on the mechanics of clathrin polymerisation, vesicle adhesion and mitochondrial fission. The lab works closely with translational early-detection teams at MD Anderson on biomarker discovery, validation in large screening cohorts, and the design of screening studies.
